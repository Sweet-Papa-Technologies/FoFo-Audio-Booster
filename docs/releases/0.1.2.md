# FoFoBooster 0.1.2

## Now Playing

- The panel subscribes to output-activity changes for each audio process, so an existing app starting or pausing playback updates the list immediately.
- The discovery fallback and meters keep updating during menu interaction and slider dragging.
- Apps with saved boost, mute, or solo controls stay reachable when their output becomes idle. Idle rows are labeled.
- Playback-state updates do not restart taps or the plugin worker. Core Audio reports open output streams, so some apps can still appear active while their player is paused; this is an app control list, not song metadata.

## More expressive visualizers

- Ember: layered glowing spectrum ridges, warm valleys, and rising sparks.
- Halo: concentric spectra, bass-driven expansion, radial detail, and a softly lit core.
- Tide: a denser time-domain waveform, broad flowing ribbons, reflection, and drifting phosphor trails.
- Grid: illuminated spectrum cells, peak markers, a perspective floor, and horizon reflections.
- Drift: 512 particles at full quality, multiple depths, flowing nebula backgrounds, and motion driven by the audio.

The analyzer separates bass, mids, treble, level, and transients; quiet tracks have stronger visual presence without changing listening volume. Waveform resolution increases from 64 to 256 points. All features share the output-latency delay, and silence or missing callbacks fade cleanly. Reduce Motion remains supported. Controls can be hidden for a cleaner view.

Opening the visualizer reuses an existing master/plugin route instead of restarting the sound. At most two GPU frames can be queued, and adaptive quality remains available under sustained load.

## Validation

30 Swift regression tests passed, including eight new app-activity and signal-analysis cases. The DSP, isolated plugin failure/state, shader compilation, and metadata suites passed locally. All five production scenes were rendered at 3840×2160 in both appearances with silence and synthetic music; checks verify non-flat output, material response to audio, and opaque compositing. The latest M4 Pro run measured a worst GPU frame of 1.12 ms. These are offscreen shader measurements, excluding the FFT, window compositor, and audio engine; they do not certify other hardware or every live playback app.

Review frames can be generated without audio or screen capture:

```sh
build/FoFoBooster.app/Contents/MacOS/FoFoBooster --validate-visualizer --visualizer-snapshots build/visualizer-review
```
