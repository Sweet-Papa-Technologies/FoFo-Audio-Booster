YOU’RE THREE LITTLE STEPS AWAY.

Set up. Settle in.

The full getting-started guide ↗
  1. 01

    Make a little room.

    Download the DMG, open it, and drag FoFoBooster into Applications. It’s one app for Apple silicon and Intel.

  2. 02

    Let your sound in.

    Open the app and follow setup to allow system audio recording. That permission lets it process sound; it doesn’t save recordings.

  3. 03

    Find your level.

    Choose your output and gently raise the boost. Use the power button or ⌥⇧B to return to original audio.
